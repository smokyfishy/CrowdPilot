# Rights-holder request templates (DRAFTS — nothing has been sent)

Outreach needs the owner's explicit approval, the owner's own name and contact details, and the owner's choice of which requests to send. Placeholders are in `{braces}`.

## 1. Netlabel / artist: research use of a released track

> **Subject:** Research permission request: "{track title}" ({catalog no.})
>
> Hello {label or artist},
>
> I'm {owner name}, running a private, noncommercial research project on automatic DJ mixing (CrowdPilot). Your track "{title}" ({catalog}) appears in the DJ mix "{mix title}" by {DJ}.
>
> We would like written permission for these specific uses only:
> (a) download and private storage of the track;
> (b) waveform analysis (tempo, beats, key, structure);
> (c) aligning it to the DJ mix to estimate the DJ's fader and EQ moves;
> (d) using the derived measurements, and optionally the audio, to train research models that stay private.
>
> We will not redistribute the audio, publish excerpts, or use it commercially. You can withdraw permission at any time, and we will then delete the audio and derived data.
>
> Could you confirm, or tell me which of (a)–(d) you are comfortable with?
>
> Thank you, {owner name} · {contact}

## 2. DJ / mix label: training on a published CC BY-NC-ND mix

> **Subject:** Research use of Mixotic {no.} "{title}"
>
> Hello {Mixotic / DJ},
>
> Your mix is published under CC BY-NC-ND. We would like to confirm that private model *training* on its audio, with no redistribution, no derivatives published and no commercial use, is acceptable to you. We will credit {DJ} in any research write-up. The rest is as in template 1.

## 3. Dataset maintainers (DJ Mix Dataset / Raveform / EDM-CUE)

> **Subject:** Access to audio for {dataset} transitions (research)
>
> Hello {maintainers},
>
> We use {dataset}'s metadata under its licence. Do you, or the rights holders, provide any route to obtain the reference mixes and exact source tracks with permission for private research analysis and training? If not, could you point us to rights-cleared subsets?
